# OpenGGF 0.7 — Development summary

Status: unreleased `0.7.prerelease` on `develop` after the 0.6.20260911 release.
`master` and `release/0.6.20260911` retain the published 0.6 snapshot; `next`
opens the `0.8.prerelease` cycle.

The [0.7 roadmap](../project/v0.7-roadmap.md) completes and validates the stock
Sonic 1, Sonic 2, and locked-on Sonic 3 & Knuckles campaigns. Existing widescreen,
modding, racing, editor, loading, and audio work moves forward from `next` under
regression coverage. The Mod API remains an unpublished candidate; the rollover
does not change its descriptor or grant full-campaign compatibility claims.

See [CHANGELOG.0.7.md](../../CHANGELOG.0.7.md) for themes and the
[development ledger](v0.7-prerelease-detailed.md) for recorded checks.
Known discrepancies and audio/trace frontiers remain open unless their owning
verification records demonstrate otherwise. The [release-7 trace contract](../status/trace-scope-release-7.md)
retains strict no-regression checks rather than resetting known debt.
