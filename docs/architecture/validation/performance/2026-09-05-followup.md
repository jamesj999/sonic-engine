## Delivered changes

- `RomByteReader.fromRom` shares one immutable reader per open `Rom`. The cache
  belongs to the ROM instance, is invalidated on open/close and public writes,
  and has no static cross-game ownership. Previously issued readers retain
  their original immutable bytes. The array constructor and slices remain
  defensive. S1 art loaders using the explicit read-all/constructor idiom now
  use the shared factory too. The initial acquisition still reads and copies
  the image; subsequent acquisitions reuse it. This deliberately retains one
  ROM-sized array until close (4 MiB for the verified S3K ROM).
- CNZ palette patches are prepared once from ROM tables. Mutable counters,
  timing, priority and submission order are unchanged. Registry application
  reads packed scalar colors without allocating or exposing private arrays;
  public byte access remains defensive. Mirrored writes share immutable owned
  bytes. Rewind still serializes only mutable cycle timers/counters.
- Timing and allocation collectors use reusable primitive section accumulators.
  First-completed-frame history order, per-frame sample order, repeated-section
  accumulation and absent-section expiry are preserved. Snapshot map entries
  are retained; public section records remain immutable. Reset now also clears
  the reused display snapshot instead of returning stale prior-frame data.
- Screenshot GL readback stays on the caller/render thread. Owned image data
  transfers to a single PNG worker with at most two in-flight images, including
  the active write. Saturation skips a new request before capture and logs it.
  Shutdown allows five seconds to drain accepted writes, then interrupts the
  worker and reports discarded pending writes; an uninterruptible active write
  is daemon-owned and cannot hold process exit. PNG writes through
  `ScreenshotCapture` are serialized around STB's global flip setting.

## Limits and remaining audit opportunities

- Lossless live capture already has pooled images and double-buffered PBOs.
  Sustained encoder overload still blocks by design; mapping may wait for the
  GPU. Increasing buffers alone cannot fix sustained overload, and this change
  does not silently introduce frame dropping. Separate GPU-wait profiling and
  machine-specific encoder tuning remain useful follow-up measurements.
- Rewind checkpoint cadence remains ten gameplay ticks and sixty audio ticks.
  Its earlier [measured tradeoff](../2026-09-05-runtime-performance.md) is retained;
  changing immutable snapshot representation without object-heavy measurements
  is deferred. No claim is made that checkpoint spikes have been eliminated.
- First-time art decoding/upload and screenshot readback still perform work on
  the render/game thread. Reader reuse removes redundant I/O/copying, not ROM
  job admission, completion timing, decompression or GPU ownership boundaries.
- No trace frontier or gameplay timing contract changed. Independent reviews
  covered ROM/screenshot lifetime and profiler/palette behavior; no actionable
  defects remained at review completion.
